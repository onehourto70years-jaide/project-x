## Full Description (4000 chars max)
```
NutriOS is your personal nutrition operating system — a beautifully designed app that makes tracking what you eat, drink, and do effortless.

🍎 SMART FOOD TRACKING
Search from thousands of foods using the USDA nutritional database. Log meals by searching, scanning barcodes, or using your favorites list. See detailed breakdowns of calories, protein, carbs, fat, vitamins, and minerals for everything you eat.

💧 HYDRATION MONITORING
Track your daily water intake with quick-add buttons. Get a smart hydration goal that adapts to your body weight and activity level. View your hydration history and streaks at a glance.

📊 PROGRESS DASHBOARD
A customizable dashboard with drag-and-drop widgets shows your health score, macros, streaks, nutrient gaps, and recent meals — all in one view. Toggle widgets on or off to build your perfect dashboard.

🤖 AI NUTRITION COACH
Powered by advanced AI, NutriOS analyzes your daily intake and provides personalized insights. Ask questions about your diet, get meal suggestions, and receive smart recommendations to fill nutritional gaps.

🏆 GAMIFICATION & STREAKS
Earn badges for consistent tracking, hitting water goals, and maintaining streaks. Stay motivated with daily achievements and milestone celebrations.

📋 RECIPES & MEAL PLANNING
Build and save your own recipes with automatic per-serving nutrition calculations. Plan your meals ahead to stay on track with your goals.

⏰ SMART REMINDERS
Get timely push notifications for meals, water intake, and daily routines. Customize when and what you want to be reminded about.

🔬 MOLECULAR ENGINE
Explore the elemental composition of your food. Understand how different cooking methods affect nutrient retention. Discover which food combinations work best together.

🌙 DAILY ROUTINES
Track morning, work, workout, and evening routines. Build healthy habits with streak tracking and completion reminders.

📈 DETAILED REPORTS
View comprehensive progress charts for nutrition, hydration, weight, and routines over 7, 14, or 30 days. Share daily or weekly reports with friends, family, or your nutritionist.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
Your health data is yours. We never sell your data to advertisers. Full account deletion available at any time. All data transmitted over encrypted connections.

⚠️ DISCLAIMER
NutriOS is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. The app is designed for general wellness and nutrition tracking. Consult a healthcare professional before making dietary changes. AI insights are for informational purposes only.

Download NutriOS today and take control of your nutrition journey.
```
